About Nuage

About Nuage
  A small talking cloud: a classic chat engine of about
  100 KB, built on Cyc-like expressions of what is, with no
  model inside.
The lightest imposition
  Thirteen sources on gentle speech: the feather, face, three
  rules, tact, ceremony, indirection, a graceful way out,
  implicature, joint attention, common ground, phatic
  communion, the premise, and sympathesis. Each says what in
  Nuage carries it, and most give an echo in information
  theory.
In this tradition
  Nuage asks rather than guesses, offers rather than detours,
  and says "I made a note of it" rather than falling silent.
Great Milestones of Chat History
  DWIM, ELIZA, SHRDLU, PARRY, ADVENT, Zork, MUD, RACTER,
  SCUMM, A.L.I.C.E., SmarterChild, Inform 7, and Cleverbot,
  each with its year, its makers, and a link.
The future is cloudy (nuageux)
  A classic chat program might someday answer as precisely as
  a clock, and as generously as it was taught.
